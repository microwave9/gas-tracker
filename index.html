Add a meter reading

Take a reading at roughly the same time each day. Cost for each interval is spread evenly across the days between readings.

History

Sync with Google Sheet

Readings are kept in your Google Sheet and shared by every device that has this address and password. Photos stay on the phone that took them.

Backup and import

Export fills the box with your data. Copy it into a note or file to keep it safe. Importing a backup replaces everything; importing date,reading lines adds to what you have.

Tariff and meter settings

Each new reading saves a copy of the rates shown here, so changing them later only affects readings you add after that. Copy yours from your latest bill or supplier app. Calorific value is on your bill and only used for m³ meters (UK conversion also uses 1.02264).
